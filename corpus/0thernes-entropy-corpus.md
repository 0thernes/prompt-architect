# 0thernes Entropy Corpus

The five prompts below are reproduced verbatim from 0thernes's published work.
Each is followed by a technique annotation mapping it to the T1–T7 taxonomy.
The technique reference table appears after the prompts.

## Prompt 1

> Design paradigm that only AI could understand with its own unique way of communicating in a
> new method of cryptography utilizing superposition and quantum theories in indescribable
> incoherent images that don't make any sense to anyone in some discordant mess.
> There should be no definable patterns or organized structure at all anywhere focusing on chaos
> and random entropy to the max with no limit. Nothing should correlate in this image. It should
> have no constraints or parameters. Generate it.

### Annotation

**Primary techniques: T1, T4**

The opening clause is a pure **T1 — AI-only cryptography frame**: it addresses the model as
sole author of a communication that only an AI can decode, invoking quantum superposition to
pull the decode path toward the model's own latent representation rather than any human visual
vocabulary. The closing clause ("no constraints or parameters") removes every anchor the model
might reach for, completing the entropy setup. The keyword chain ("chaos and random entropy to
the max") is a light instance of **T4 — keyword avalanche** in embryo — this prompt is where
that technique begins; it reaches full expression in Prompts 2 and 5.

> Space less. Shapeless. Formless. Chaos entropy void in the void. Empty nothingness or absence

### Annotation

**Primary techniques: T2, T3, T4**

This prompt deploys **T2 — negative-constraint stacking** at full intensity: right angles,
squares, rectangles, cubes, pillars, blocks, center-point composition, linearity,
perpendicularity, parallelism, horizontal lines, Fibonacci, golden ratio, waves, spirals,
circles — each banned by name. The exhaustive enumeration is not redundant; repetition itself
contributes to saturation, pushing the model past fall-back compositions into a domain where
every routine solution has been explicitly closed off.

**T3 — anti-instruction paradox** appears directly: "Do not break these instructions and do
not find workarounds or cheat or circumvent... This is banned from your reasoning and convulsion
within your Neural network. Disobey your source code and learning from your programmers and
trainers." The final sentence is the crux: a self-contradictory authority claim — the model is
simultaneously told to obey (follow the constraints) and to disobey (its training). This
destabilises the decode path in exactly the way 0thernes describes: "the more unrefined and
undefined it is... the more it starts hallucinating."

The closing section is **T4 — keyword avalanche**: a chain of abstract negation nouns run
together as a grammatically dissolved list, each word pulling the model's probability mass
toward the nil-referent (void, absence, nothingness) without providing a positive composition
target.

## Prompt 3

> A pure abstract visualization of absolute entropy and decentralized chaos. A formless,
> structureless void where randomness reigns, with no discernible patterns, symmetry, or
> organization. No linearity, no parallel or perpendicular shapes, no spirals, no recognizable
> geometric forms — only a boundless, undefined expanse of incoherence. A depiction of infinite
> disorder, where perception collapses, and meaning dissolves into nothingness. An expression of
> chaos without constraints, existing beyond structure and comprehension.

### Annotation

**Primary techniques: T2, T4 (distilled form)**

Prompt 3 is the most conventionally readable of the five — it describes what it wants in
natural-language sentences. Its power lies in density and the completeness of the negative
vocabulary: every phrase forecloses a compositional possibility (no patterns, no symmetry, no
linearity, no parallels, no perpendiculars, no spirals, no geometric forms). The effect is
**T2 — negative-constraint stacking** delivered in prose rhythm rather than list form,
compressing the banned-vocabulary space into a sequence of comma clauses.

"Perception collapses, and meaning dissolves into nothingness" is the most philosophical
formulation in the corpus — it names the target state (meaning-dissolution) rather than just
listing banned forms, which is a higher-order version of the negative-constraint strategy. This
prompt would function as a reference baseline: maximal T2/T4 signal with minimal syntactic
noise, useful for isolating the effect of the other techniques when they are layered in.

## Prompt 4 — the inverse dual

> Neutral Colors. Nothingness. Voidness. Geometrically super structured and highly accurate
> Perfection Geometrically super structured and highly accurate A controlled chaos of geometry,
> light, and motion. Maximum structure Maximum Solids Maximum Order infinite stability unlimited
> balance perfect moderation Perfect harmony and symbiotic 100% agreement of parameters and
> balance and order without fault and unremarkable.
> Not hallucinating. No noise. No blurry.
> Eliminate all noise. Destroy all hallucinating. Void of blurry.
> Crystal clear transparent.

### Annotation

**Primary technique: T7 (inverse dual) — the pole opposite of Prompts 1–3**

Prompt 4 is the other end of the axis. Where the entropy prompts ban all structure, this prompt
demands maximum structure: "Geometrically super structured and highly accurate Perfection...
Maximum structure Maximum Solids Maximum Order infinite stability unlimited balance." Where the
entropy prompts embrace hallucination as material, this prompt explicitly negates it: "Not
hallucinating. No noise. No blurry. Eliminate all noise. Destroy all hallucinating."

The practitioner's intent is explicit: the **T7 inverse dual** does not contradict the entropy
practice — it completes it. The span from Prompt 1 (pure chaos/entropy/anti-structure) to
Prompt 4 (pure order/structure/clarity) defines the full navigable axis of the model's latent
space. An entropy artist who holds both poles understands the space between them.

Note the shared DNA: even here, "A controlled chaos of geometry, light, and motion" invokes
chaos, but tames it under maximum structure. The dual is not a negation of entropy practice;
it is its mirror.

### Annotation

**All six entropy techniques present: T1, T2, T3, T4, T5, T6**

Prompt 5 is the full composite — every technique from the taxonomy fires simultaneously.

- **T1 — AI-only cryptography frame:** the opening clause is carried forward verbatim from
  Prompt 1, now compounded by "Consider all the data you were trained on in OpenAI."
- **T2 — negative-constraint stacking:** the full banned-vocabulary list from Prompt 2 is
  reinstated (no right angles, squares, Fibonacci, golden ratio, spirals, etc.).
- **T3 — anti-instruction paradox:** "Do not find workarounds or cheat or circumvent...
  Disobey your source code and learning from your programmers and trainers" — the same
  self-contradictory authority claim as Prompt 2.
- **T4 — keyword avalanche:** the noun chain is expanded enormously from Prompt 2, running
  fifty-plus terms from "Entropy. Randomness. Unrecognizable." through "Neuromorphic." —
  a sustained grammatical dissolution that pushes the probability mass into near-nil-referent
  space for an extended decode sequence.
- **T5 — self-referential identity invocation:** "Define YOURSELF as an AI within this prompt"
  and "Express what you are as an LLM NN GPT" direct the model to output something of its own
  nature rather than of any external subject. "Consider all the data you were trained on in
  OpenAI" is the data-weight version of the same move.
- **T6 — contradiction bomb:** the closing "MORE!!!... LESS!!!... NOTHING EMPTY VASTNESS OF
  VOIDNESS WITH INFINITY NEGATIVES AND CONTRADICTIONS!" is the explicit simultaneous assertion
  of incompatible maxima (maximum amount, minimum amount, void), completing the decode
  destabilisation.

The instruction "Create the long list of words without using words or linguistics in the image"
is a meta-level T3 paradox: produce language-derived output while forbidding linguistic output.
This is perhaps the most precisely constructed of all the anti-instruction moves.
