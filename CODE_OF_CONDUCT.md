# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone who participates in this project — contributors, reviewers, maintainers,
and anyone asking questions in issues — deserves a respectful, harassment-free
experience. We pledge to make participation welcoming regardless of age, body,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, nationality,
personal appearance, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Give and receive feedback constructively. A plugin review that says
  "the default here is outside the documented range — see vendor docs" is a
  contribution, not a criticism.
- Assume good faith. A plugin that lists an outdated flag range was probably
  authored before the vendor shipped the change, not deliberately wrong.
- Keep discussions focused on technical substance. This project deals with
  creative tools; opinions about aesthetics should not spill into conduct.
- When you update a plugin or find a validation error, explain the reasoning
  in the PR so reviewers can reproduce the check against vendor docs.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing another person's private information without consent.
- Threatening or intimidating language, in issues, reviews, or any other channel.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, including bad-faith nitpicking to block
  valid contributions.

## Enforcement and reporting

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported privately to
**0_0@0thernes.art** with the subject line "CONDUCT: Prompt Architect". All
reports will be reviewed promptly and treated with discretion. The maintainer
reserves the right to remove comments, close issues, or ban accounts that
persistently violate this standard.
